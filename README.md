# Waiting for the Holidays with Youth AI Lab

An online advent calendar: one Youth AI Lab resource per day from 1 to 23 December, and an AI Christmas quiz on 24 December.

- `index.html`: the calendar. Doors open on their date. Add `?preview=all` to open every door, or `?date=2026-12-08` to see a given day.
- `quiz.html`: the AI Christmas Quiz (door 24).

Co-funded by the European Union. Resources under CC BY-SA 4.0; photo credits are listed in the quiz.
